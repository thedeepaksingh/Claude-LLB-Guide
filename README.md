# Claude-LLB-Guide

Study material and exam preparation for **LL.B. (3-Year) Semester I — Ch. Charan Singh University (CCSU), Meerut**, prepared for IEC Law College students.

Each paper is written as a full revision pack: background, evolution, core concepts, leading cases with citations, old and current statutory sections, criticism, current developments, and model question-and-answer sets, so a topic can be answered from first principles in the exam.

## Important

- Law is stated **as on the date in each file's header**. Before the exam, verify any case you plan to cite on SCC Online, Manupatra or Indian Kanoon.
- Where your teacher's class notes differ from this material, follow your teacher for the exam.
- This is study material, not legal advice.
