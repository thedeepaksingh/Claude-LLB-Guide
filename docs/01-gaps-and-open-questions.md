## B. Currency issues in the syllabus itself (law has moved on)

1. **IPC → Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita, 2023 (BNS).** The syllabus for Paper IV still says "Indian Penal Code". The BNS came into force on 1 July 2024 and replaced the IPC for new offences. Two companion statutes also changed: the Bharatiya Nagarik Suraksha Sanhita (replacing CrPC) and the Bharatiya Sakshya Adhiniyam (replacing the Evidence Act). Paper IV material will give **IPC section → BNS section** side by side so you can answer whichever the examiner expects.
2. **"Sedition" is listed in Paper IV, Unit 4.** IPC s. 124A was kept in abeyance by the Supreme Court in 2022 (*S.G. Vombatkere v. Union of India*) and the BNS has no section called sedition; it has a different offence on acts endangering sovereignty, unity and integrity of India. Answers should cover all three layers: IPC history, the 2022 order, and the BNS replacement.
3. **"Adultery" is listed in Paper IV, Unit 4.** IPC s. 497 was struck down in *Joseph Shine v. Union of India* (2018) and the BNS does not reintroduce it. It stays in the syllabus as a topic to be answered with that history.
4. **Consumer Protection Act — year not stated** in Paper III. The 1986 Act was repealed and replaced by the **Consumer Protection Act, 2019** (most provisions in force from 20 July 2020). Paper III answers will be written on the 2019 Act with the 1986 background.
5. **Constitution (Paper II).** Amendments and landmark rulings since older textbooks (e.g. the right to privacy, EWS reservation, women's reservation amendment) need to be reflected. Each Paper II unit will carry a "post-2017 developments" box.

## D. Honest limits

- **Full marks cannot be guaranteed by any study material.** Marks depend on how you write, on the evaluator, and on the paper. This repo aims for complete coverage and examiner-friendly structure.
- **Citations must be checked.** I cite from memory plus web verification; law reports (SCC, AIR) have occasional discrepancies. Before the exam, check the cases you plan to quote on SCC Online, Manupatra or Indian Kanoon. Each file carries a verification checklist.
- **Textbook-specific wording** (what your teachers dictated in class) is not in here. Where your teacher's notes differ from this repo, follow your teacher for the exam and tell me so I can align.
