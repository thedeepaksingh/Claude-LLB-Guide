## Dec 2025 (series (20126), printed pages 4)
A (4 marks each)
1. Explain with example, the term "Voidable Contract".
2. "Acceptance must be absolute and unqualified." Explain.
3. (Problem) 'A' contract with 'B' to kill 'C' for a sum of rupees one Lakh. 'B' kills 'C'. 'A' refuses to pay. Can 'B' recover the amount? Decide.
4. What is Fraud?
5. Write a note on General offer.
B (10 marks each, any 2 of 3)
6. State the different kinds of mistake and explain their effect on contracts.
7. Insufficiency of consideration is immaterial but an agreement without consideration is void. Comment.
8. Explain "Wagering agreements".
C (20 marks each, any 3 of 5)
9. What damages can be claimed in case of the breach of contract? Discuss in detail.
10. What do you understand by Contingent Contract? Discuss the rules relating to such contracts.
11. Explain the essentials of a valid proposal with the help of Case Law.
12. Discuss the provisions of Indian Contract Act relating to Quasi Contractual obligations.
13. Discuss the contractual liability of a minor. Can a person after attaining majority ratify an agreement made by him during his minority?
