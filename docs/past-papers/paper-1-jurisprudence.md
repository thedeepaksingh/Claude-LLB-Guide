## Dec 2025 (series (20126), printed pages 7)
A (4 marks each)
1. Write the economic definition of Law given by Karl Marx.
2. How Kelsen was influenced by Austin?
3. What is the meaning of Legal Positivism?
4. Name the books written by Sir Henry Maine and Savigny. Two each.
5. Contribution of Leon Duguit.
B (10 marks each, ≤200 words, any 2 of 3)
6. Evaluate the need for the study of Jurisprudence.
7. Is it correct to say that Pure Theory of Law has left dry bones of law, deprived of flesh and blood, which give them life? Explain.
8. "The prophecies of what the courts will do in fact, and nothing more pretentious, are what I mean by the Law." Discuss.
C (20 marks each, any 3 of 5)
9. According to Austin, Jurisprudence can be divided into 'General and Particular.' To what extent do Holland and Salmond agree with this view?
10. The movement of progressive societies has hitherto been a movement from status to contract. Comment on this statement and show how far it is applicable in India?
11. The aim of social engineering is to build an efficient structure of society as possible, which involves the balancing of competing interests. Discuss.
12. "Law is the body of principles, recognized and applied by the state in the administration of Justice." Discuss.
13. The basis of natural law was the man's reason and wisdom and not the spiritual power of God. Discuss.
