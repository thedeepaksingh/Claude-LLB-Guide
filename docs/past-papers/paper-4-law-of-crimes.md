## Dec 2025 (printed paper code 11313, series (20126), Printed Pages 4; title "Law of Crimes-I (Bhartiya Nyay Sanhita-2023) (K-1006)")
A (4 marks each, <=75 words)
1. Define 'Solitary Confinement'.
2. What do you mean by 'Dowry Death'?
3. What do you mean by 'Mischief'?
4. Cheating by Personation.
5. Differentiate between 'Theft and Misappropriation'.
B (10 marks each, <=200 words, any 2 of 3)
6. Define crime. Discuss different stages of a crime.
7. What is extortion? When does extortion amount to dacoity?
8. What is wrongful restraint? Distinguish it from wrongful confinement.
C (20 marks each, any 3 of 5)
9. Discuss Right of Private Defence of property. When does it extend up to causing death?
10. What are the essential elements that constitute an unlawful assembly under the Bhartiya Nyay Sanhita, 2023? How does the law differentiate between Lawful and Unlawful gatherings?
11. When does culpable Homicide amount to murder?
12. Define kidnapping. How does it differ from abduction? Can there be an abduction which is not punishable under BNS?
13. Discuss fully the law relating to constructive criminality.

## Dec 2024 (printed paper code 11313, series (21224), "Printed Pages: 7"; title "Law of Crimes (Bhartiya Nyay Sanhita-2023) (K-1006)")
NOTE: first paper in this series printed on the BNS; printed code is K-1006 / 11313 (not K-1004 / 11203) -- flag in docs.
A (4 marks each, <=75 words)
1. Legal Insanity and medical Insanity
2. Actus non facit reum nisi mens sit rea
3. Cheating
4. Abettor
5. Stalking
B (10 marks each, <=200 words, any 2 of 3)
6. What are the essential elements that constitute an unlawful assembly under the Bharatiya Nyay Sanhita, 2023? How does the law differentiate between lawful and unlawful gatherings?
7. What is the concept of crime in criminal law? What are the essential elements that must be present for an act or omission to be considered as a crime?
8. Define Kidnapping in the context of Bharatiya Nyay Sanhita, 2023? Explain how does Kidnapping differ from abduction?
C (20 marks each, any 3 of 5)
9. Define theft as per the Bharatiya Nyay Sanhita, 2023? How does theft differ from the Extortion? Explain with illustrations.
10. What is the distinction between culpable homicide and murder under the Bharatiya Nyay Sanhita, 2023? What are the key factors that differentiate these two? Explain with the help of decided case laws.
11. Define Dowry Death in the context of the Bharatiya Nyay Sanhita, 2023. How does it provide dowry death differently from the Indian Penal Code, 1860?
12. What are the different stages of crime under the criminal law? How does the criminal law address each stage in terms of liability and punishment?
13. Comment on any two of these three of the following: (a) Constructive Liability under the criminal law. (b) Offences against child under the Bharatiya Nyay Sanhita, 2023. (c) Right of Private Defence.

## Dec 2023 (series (21223), printed pages 4; title "I.P.C. (Law of Crime)")

1. Movable Property
2. Injury
3. Strict liability
4. Wrongful loss
5. Good faith
B (10 marks each, any 2 of 3)
6. What are the ingredients of the offence of theft? Explain them and illustrate.

12. Describe the scope of 'Intention', 'Preparation' and 'attempt' to commit a crime.
13. Distinguish between: (a) 'Rioting' and 'Affray' (b) Wrongful Restraint and wrongful confinement.
