## Dec 2025 (Paper code 11201, Roll No. series (20126), Printed Pages 4)
A (4 marks each, ≤75 words)
1. Explain in very brief those terms which were added in Constitution by 42nd Constitutional Amendment, 1976.
2. Write down any four fundamental duties as prescribed under Indian Constitution.
3. Against whom Fundamental Rights are available? Explain in very brief.
4. What do you mean by protection from 'Ex-post facto laws'?
5. Under what conditions Fundamental Rights can be suspended?
B (10 marks each, ≤200 words, any 2 of 3)
6. What do you mean by Federalism? Is Indian Constitution federal? Discuss.
7. Doctrine of reasonableness and doctrine of non-arbitrariness are the guiding principles of equality clause. Discuss with the help of decided cases.
8. Write a critical note on Cultural and Educational Rights of Minorities as prescribed under Indian Constitution. Refer to decided cases.
C (20 marks each, any 3 of 5)
9. With the help of decided cases discuss expanding horizons of freedom of speech and expression as prescribed under Indian Constitution.
10. Right to life does not mean mere animal existence but it means right to live with human dignity. Discuss with the help of decided cases.
11. Public Interest Litigation is a prominent tool to get constitutional remedies now a days. Discuss with the help of decided cases.
12. With the help of decided cases discuss the relationship between Directive Principles of State Policy and Fundamental Rights.
13. Write short notes on: (a) Protection against self incrimination (b) Doctrine of double jeopardy.
