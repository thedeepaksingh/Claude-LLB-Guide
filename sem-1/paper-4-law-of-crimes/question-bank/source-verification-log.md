> **What this is.** An honest list of what in the Paper IV question bank was **checked against a source**, what was **changed** because a check or a re-read showed an error, and what is **still unchecked**. Read section 4 before you rely on any exact section number, citation, date or fact of a case.
> **How to read "checked".** I read a source for the point during this project (the text of the Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita, 2023 or a summary of it on the web, a report of an order, or a news report) in October 2026. **I did not keep a URL for each point**, so where it matters in the exam, confirm from the bare Act or the official report. In the notes and answers, **‡** marks a point I checked this way and **†** marks a point written from memory that I could not check. An unmarked point is standard textbook knowledge that I did not re-check.
> **The most important limit.** The BNS has 358 sections and I did not read all of them. The sections marked ‡ below were read; every other BNS number in the bank is either marked † or follows from one that was read.

## 4. Not checked: verify before you rely on these

**A. Law and events that may have moved, or that I could not confirm**
1. **The paper code.** The syllabus gives K-1004 (and the syllabus map code 11203), but the 2024 and 2025 papers print K-1006 / 11313 and are set on the BNS. I could not find out whether K-1006 is a new code for the same paper or a different paper. The contents of the papers show the same subject.
2. **Abetment and conspiracy section numbers.** BNS ss. 45–60 (abetment), s. 61 (conspiracy) and s. 62 (attempt) were worked from the order of the IPC (ss. 107–120 and 511), for example 49 = IPC s. 109, 51 = IPC s. 111, 54 = IPC s. 114, 55 = IPC s. 115, 56 = IPC s. 116. Treat them as †.
3. **BNS ss. 93–99 (offences against children).** I read the headings but **not the punishments**. A-99…A-101 give the IPC terms with a †. BNS s. 95 (hiring a child to commit an offence) is new; its punishment is unchecked.
4. **Other BNS sections marked †:** ss. 119–123, 125, 110, 132–136, 139, 140(3)–(4), 141–142, 305–307 (aggravated theft), 311–315, 317, 320–323, 325–329 and 332–334 (sub-sections), and ss. 329–330 (criminal trespass, house-trespass, house-breaking); s. 105 (the wording of its second limb); s. 22 (the exact wording); s. 38 (the fourth limb, "unnatural lust"); s. 3(5) (common intention); ss. 4, 6, 8 (punishments, fractions, fine).
5. **Rape provisions.** BNS ss. 63, 65, 70 and 71 and the exception for a wife not under eighteen are †. Status of the Supreme Court challenge to the marital-rape exception: **not checked**.
6. **BNS s. 69** (sexual intercourse by deceitful means): the section exists ‡; its punishment is †.
7. **Hit and run (s. 106(2)):** whether it has been brought into force after July 2025.
8. **Sedition:** whether the Supreme Court has decided on s. 124A or s. 152 since 22 May 2026. The news item on s. 85 (cruelty) that I saw was not read.
9. **BNSS and BSA equivalents.** BSA ss. 8, 108, 117, 118 and BNSS ss. 35, 148–150, 163, 359, 367–369, 393(3), 407 are from memory †.
10. **Adultery.** The Parliamentary Standing Committee's recommendation of November 2023 to restore a gender-neutral adultery offence is from memory †. I did not check whether Parliament has acted.
11. **Community service.** Where it is defined, which offences carry it, and the rules for it: **not checked**.
12. **Amendments to the BNS after 2024.** I found none, but I did not search the Gazette.
13. **IPC "511 sections, 23 chapters".** This is the commonly stated figure; I did not count.
14. Any change since 29 October 2026 in the law.
