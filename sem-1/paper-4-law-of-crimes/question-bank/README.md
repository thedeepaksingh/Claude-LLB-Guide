## 5. How to use the bank

1. **Learn the framework first.** Read the [digest](cases-and-statutes-digest.md) Part 3 (topic to cases) and write each leading case with its year, facts in one line and holding from memory. Learn the BNS section numbers for the offences you will write about, with the IPC number in brackets.
2. **Section A (compulsory, 20 marks).** Read the 135 four-mark answers at least twice. They are all 75 words or fewer, so each takes four to five minutes to write. Practise the definitions that keep returning: mischief, cheating (and by personation), theft and misappropriation, dowry death, grievous hurt, good faith, dishonestly, movable property, injury, wrongful loss, criminal force, bigamy, stalking.
3. **Section B (any two, 20 marks).** Learn at least twelve of the 60 ten-mark answers well; they are written to fit 200 words. Practise the **problem** (B-49) in IRAC form.
4. **Section C (any three, 60 marks).** Prepare at least ten of the 25 twenty-mark answers: C-12 and C-13 (culpable homicide and murder), C-05 (ss. 34 and 149), C-02 (stages of crime), C-19 and C-20 (theft, extortion, robbery), C-15 and C-16 (kidnapping, abduction, restraint), C-08 and C-09 (private defence), C-23 (sedition and s. 152), C-24 (unlawful assembly, riot, affray), C-01 (crime).
5. **Test yourself.** Sit the [three mock papers](mock-exam-1.md) and mark with the [checklist](answer-review-checklist.md).
6. **The day before.** Do the currency check in the checklist (especially sedition and BNS s. 152, hit and run, and the BNS section numbers marked †) and re-read the Section A answers.

## 6. What is not here

- **The university's marking scheme.** I could not find one. The mark splits are my own estimate of what an examiner looks for.
- **Offences outside the syllabus,** such as rape and sexual offences in full, offences relating to coins, documents and forgery, defamation (a Paper III topic) and the new organised-crime and terrorism offences; they are mentioned only where they touch a named topic.
- **Hindi-medium answers.** The questions are bilingual in print; the answers are in English only.
- **Section numbers I could not check.** Several BNS numbers are marked † because I worked them from the IPC order or from memory; see the [log](source-verification-log.md). In the exam, write the IPC number as well.
