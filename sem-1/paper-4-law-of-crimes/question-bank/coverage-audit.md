## 7. How this audit was checked

A script (run on the final files) did the following, and stopped at any failure:

1. Parsed every answer anchor and confirmed that A-01…A-135, B-01…B-60 and C-01…C-25 each exist exactly once.
2. Read each printed word count (exactly one per answer) and confirmed A ≤ 75 and B ≤ 200.
3. Confirmed that all 52 past questions are in the transcription and map to an answer of the right section.
4. Confirmed that every answer appears in at least one syllabus line (no orphan answers) and that every line has an answer at each of the three levels.
5. Confirmed that the topic-frequency table covers exactly the 52 questions (55 parts).
6. Rebuilt this file and the [README](README.md) from those results.

A separate link checker resolves every relative link and anchor in the Paper IV folder and in `docs/` (see section 6 of the [log](source-verification-log.md)).
