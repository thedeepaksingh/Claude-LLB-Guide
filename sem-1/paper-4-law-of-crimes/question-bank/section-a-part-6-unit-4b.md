<a id="a-102"></a>
**A-102 · Theft** · *Related: B-48, C-19*

BNS s. 303(1) (IPC s. 378): whoever, intending to take dishonestly any movable property out of the possession of any person without that person's consent, moves that property in order to such taking, commits theft ‡. Punishment: up to three years, or fine, or both; a later conviction: rigorous imprisonment of one to five years and fine (s. 303(2)) ‡.

*Word count: 58*

<a id="a-103"></a>
**A-103 · Snatching** · *Related: B-14, C-19*

BNS s. 304 (new): theft is snatching if, in order to commit theft, the offender suddenly or quickly or forcibly seizes, secures, grabs or takes away any movable property from any person or from his possession. Punishment: imprisonment up to three years, and fine ‡. Example: snatching a necklace from a woman on the road.

*Word count: 54*

<a id="a-104"></a>
**A-104 · Theft and misappropriation** · *Asked: Dec 2025 Q5* · *Related: B-52, C-19, C-21*

In theft (BNS s. 303) property is taken out of another's possession without consent, with dishonest intention at the time of moving. In dishonest misappropriation (s. 314; IPC s. 403) possession is obtained innocently, as by finding, and dishonesty arises later; no moving is needed. Example: a ring on a table in Z's house is theft; a ring found on the road and kept is misappropriation †.

*Word count: 66*

<a id="a-105"></a>
**A-105 · Extortion** · *Related: B-50, C-20*

BNS s. 308(1) (IPC s. 383): whoever intentionally puts any person in fear of any injury to that person or another, and thereby dishonestly induces the person so put in fear to deliver any property, valuable security, or anything signed or sealed which may be converted into a valuable security, commits extortion ‡. Punishment: up to seven years, or fine, or both (s. 308(2)) ‡.

*Word count: 63*

<a id="a-106"></a>
**A-106 · Robbery** · *Related: B-50, B-51, C-20*

BNS s. 309 (IPC s. 390): in all robbery there is either theft or extortion ‡. Theft is robbery if, to commit it or in carrying away the property, the offender voluntarily causes or attempts to cause death, hurt or wrongful restraint, or fear of instant death, hurt or restraint. Punishment: rigorous imprisonment up to ten years and fine; up to fourteen years on a highway between sunset and sunrise (s. 309(4)) ‡.

*Word count: 71*

<a id="a-107"></a>
**A-107 · Dacoity** · *Related: B-50, B-51, C-20*

BNS s. 310(1) (IPC s. 391): when five or more persons conjointly commit or attempt to commit a robbery, or where those committing it and those present and aiding amount to five or more, every one of them commits dacoity ‡. Punishment: life imprisonment, or rigorous imprisonment up to ten years, and fine (s. 310(2)). If one commits murder, every member is liable (s. 310(3)) ‡.

*Word count: 64*

<a id="a-108"></a>
**A-108 · Robbery and dacoity** · *Related: B-51, C-20*

Dacoity is robbery committed by five or more persons conjointly (BNS ss. 309, 310); in robbery the number is immaterial. Robbery: rigorous imprisonment up to ten years (fourteen on a highway at night); dacoity: life, or up to ten years. If murder is committed in the course of dacoity, every dacoit is liable to death, life, or at least ten years (s. 310(3)) ‡.

*Word count: 63*

<a id="a-109"></a>
**A-109 · Criminal misappropriation** · *Related: B-52, C-21*

BNS s. 314 (IPC s. 403): whoever dishonestly misappropriates or converts to his own use any movable property commits criminal misappropriation †. Possession is obtained innocently, as by finding the property; the dishonesty arises later. Punishment: six months to two years and fine †. Example: keeping a ring found lying on the road.

*Word count: 51*

<a id="a-110"></a>
**A-110 · Criminal breach of trust** · *Related: B-52, C-21*

BNS s. 316(1) (IPC s. 405): whoever, being entrusted with property or with any dominion over it, dishonestly misappropriates or converts it to his own use, or dishonestly uses or disposes of it in violation of a direction of law or a legal contract about the trust, or wilfully suffers another to do so, commits criminal breach of trust ‡. Punishment: up to five years, or fine, or both (s. 316(2)) ‡.

*Word count: 70*

<a id="a-111"></a>
**A-111 · Criminal breach of trust and misappropriation** · *Related: B-52, C-21*

Both involve dishonest conversion of property. In misappropriation (BNS s. 314) the property comes into the accused's hands innocently, without entrustment. In criminal breach of trust (s. 316) it is entrusted to him, or he has dominion over it. Breach of trust is punished more heavily: up to five years, and life or up to ten years for a banker, agent or public servant ‡.

*Word count: 64*

<a id="a-112"></a>
**A-112 · Cheating** · *Asked: Dec 2024 Q3* · *Related: B-53, C-21*

BNS s. 318(1) (IPC s. 415): whoever, by deceiving any person, fraudulently or dishonestly induces him to deliver any property, or to consent that any person retain it, or intentionally induces him to do or omit what he would not do if not deceived, causing or likely to cause damage or harm in body, mind, reputation or property, cheats ‡. Punishment: up to three years, or fine, or both (s. 318(2)) ‡.

*Word count: 70*

<a id="a-113"></a>
**A-113 · Cheating by personation** · *Asked: Dec 2025 Q4* · *Related: B-53, C-21*

BNS s. 319 (IPC s. 416): a person cheats by personation if he cheats by pretending to be some other person, or by knowingly substituting one person for another, or representing that he or any other person is a person other than he really is. The offence is committed whether the person personated is real or imaginary. Punishment: up to five years, or fine, or both ‡.

*Word count: 66*

<a id="a-114"></a>
**A-114 · Mischief** · *Asked: Dec 2025 Q3* · *Related: B-54, C-22*

BNS s. 324(1) (IPC s. 425): whoever, with intent to cause, or knowing that he is likely to cause, wrongful loss or damage to the public or any person, destroys any property, or changes it or its situation so as to destroy or diminish its value or utility or to affect it injuriously, commits mischief ‡. Punishment: up to six months, or fine, or both (s. 324(2)) ‡.

*Word count: 66*

<a id="a-115"></a>
**A-115 · Criminal trespass** · *Related: B-54, C-22*

BNS s. 329(1) (IPC s. 441): whoever enters into or upon property in the possession of another with intent to commit an offence, or to intimidate, insult or annoy the person in possession, or, having lawfully entered, unlawfully remains there with that intent, commits criminal trespass †. Entry without such intent is a civil trespass only.

*Word count: 55*

<a id="a-116"></a>
**A-116 · House-trespass and house-breaking** · *Related: B-49, B-54, C-22*

House-trespass (BNS s. 329(2); IPC s. 442) is criminal trespass in a building used as a dwelling, a place of worship or a place for the custody of property. House-breaking (s. 330; IPC s. 445) is house-trespass by one of six modes of entry, such as scaling a wall, opening a lock or using force †. Punishments are in s. 331 ‡.

*Word count: 60*

<a id="a-117"></a>
**A-117 · Lurking house-trespass** · *Related: B-49, B-54, C-22*

BNS s. 330 (IPC s. 443): lurking house-trespass is house-trespass after taking precautions to conceal it from the person who has a right to exclude or eject the trespasser †. Punishment (s. 331(1), (2)) ‡: up to two years and fine; up to three years if committed after sunset and before sunrise.

*Word count: 50*

<a id="a-118"></a>
**A-118 · Theft and extortion** · *Related: B-50, C-19 (set at 20 marks in Dec 2024 Q9)*

In theft (BNS s. 303) property is taken without the owner's consent and there is no fear; in extortion (s. 308) consent is obtained by putting the owner in fear of injury, and the victim delivers the thing. Theft concerns movable property only; extortion concerns any property or valuable security. Punishment: theft up to three years; extortion up to seven ‡.

*Word count: 60*

<a id="a-119"></a>
**A-119 · Aggravated theft** · *Related: B-48, C-19*

BNS ss. 305–307 (IPC ss. 380–382) †: theft in a dwelling house, means of transport or place of worship (up to seven years and fine); theft by a clerk or servant of the master's property (up to seven years and fine); and theft after preparation for causing death, hurt or restraint (rigorous imprisonment up to ten years and fine).

*Word count: 58*
