> **What this is.** An honest list of what in the Paper III question bank was **checked against a source**, what was **changed** because a check or a re-read showed an error, and what is **still unchecked**. Read section 4 before you rely on any exact section number, citation, date or fact of a case.
> **How to read "checked".** I read a source for the point during this project (the text of an Act or Rules, a report of a judgment, or a news report) in October 2026. **I did not keep a URL for each point**, so where it matters in the exam, confirm from the bare Act or the official report. In the notes and answers, **‡** marks a point I checked this way and **†** marks a point written from memory that I could not confirm; an unmarked point is standard textbook knowledge. **Most of the English case law in this paper is from memory:** the case name, year and holding are reliable, the report reference and some of the facts are not.
