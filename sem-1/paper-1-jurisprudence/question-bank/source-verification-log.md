# Paper I · Source Verification Log

> **What this is.** An honest list of what in the Paper I question bank was **checked against a source**, what was **changed** because a check or a re-read showed an error, and what is **still unchecked**. Read section 4 before you rely on any exact date, work title, citation or vote count.
> **How to read "checked".** I read a source for the point during this project (a judgment, a report, or a textbook-style summary on the web) on 27 or 28 October 2026. I did not keep a URL for each point, so where it matters in the exam, confirm from the book or the official report. **Most of Paper I is jurisprudence, which has no "official text" to check against.** The thinkers' definitions and theories come from standard textbooks and the original works as widely quoted; I could check some of them only in secondary sources. "Not checked" means I wrote the point from memory or from the older unit notes and did not confirm it.
