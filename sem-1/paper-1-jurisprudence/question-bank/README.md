Treat the top of the table as the first things to master, then work through the rest of the syllabus.

## 5. How to use the bank

1. **Learn the jurists first.** Read Part 1 of the [digest](jurists-and-cases-digest.md) and write each thinker's name, work, year, core idea and one criticism from memory.
2. **Section A (compulsory, 20 marks).** Read the 110 four-mark answers at least twice. They are all 75 words or fewer, so each takes four to five minutes to write. Practise the names that keep returning: Manu, Bentham, Duguit, Holmes, Kelsen.
3. **Section B (any two, 20 marks).** Learn at least ten of the 48 ten-mark answers well; know that they are written to fit 200 words.
4. **Section C (any three, 60 marks).** Prepare at least eight of the 24 twenty-mark answers (the first eight in the frequency table above). Write each in 30–35 minutes with the headings, the thinkers' works and one Indian case.
5. **Test yourself.** Sit the [three mock papers](mock-exam-1.md) and mark with the [checklist](answer-review-checklist.md).
6. **The day before.** Do the currency check in the checklist and re-read the Section A answers.
